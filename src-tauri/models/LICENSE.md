# OCR model attribution

`text-detection.rten` and `text-recognition.rten` are the OCR models published by Robert Knight for [ocrs](https://huggingface.co/robertknight/ocrs). They are distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). No changes were made to the model files.

The app downloads these exact model files on request and stores them in the app data dependencies folder. Once downloaded, image and scanned document OCR works offline.
